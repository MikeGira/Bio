Last updated: 2026-07-12

This site is the personal portfolio of Michael Twagirayezu (Toronto, Ontario, Canada). It collects only the limited data described below, and only when you choose to use a feature.

Data is handled only by service providers acting on my instructions. I do not sell personal data.

3. Where it's processed

These providers process data in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on the appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) offered by each provider.

4. Cookies & local storage

This site stores your theme preference and a few non-identifying values in your browser's local storage so your settings persist between visits. It does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

5. Your rights & erasure

You can ask me to access, correct, or delete any personal data this site holds about you. To unsubscribe from updates, use the link in any email. For everything else, including erasure of your contact submission or subscriber record, email byosekumbuga@gmail.com and I will take care of it.
